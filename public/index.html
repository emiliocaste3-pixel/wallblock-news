Friday close · Nov 20 · Updated Nov 21, 2026

S&P 500 and Dow close in correction; Nasdaq erases its 2026 gain

Wall Street ended its worst week since April 2025 in a rout. On Friday the S&P 500 fell 3.7% to 6,910.39, its seventh straight loss (−8.6% on the week), and the Dow lost 1.93% to 48,147.33, both closing in correction territory. The Nasdaq Composite dropped 5.28% to 22,617.73 and has erased its 2026 gain. Nvidia lost about 14%, and markets price a December Fed hike as near-certain. U.S. markets are closed Thursday for Thanksgiving.

Wall StreetNov 20, 2026

Volatility jumps and money rushes into bonds

The VIX volatility index surged, with reports putting it between about 34 and 37 (sources differ on the close). Treasury yields fell as investors sought safety: the 10-year ended near 4.94%, about 10 basis points lower. Analysts pointed to doubts about AI spending, hawkish Fed comments and a report of an SEC review of Nvidia's accounting.

Sources: Kitco, Motley Fool, Investrade

US PoliticsNov 20, 2026

Trump says rates are too high because the Fed is “political”

At a Friday news conference Trump said interest rates are too high because the Fed is “political”, though he said he does not blame Kevin Warsh, his pick to succeed Powell. He argued a one-point cut would save about $650 billion in debt costs. Markets are betting on the opposite: a quarter-point hike in December.

Sources: Reuters via TradingView, The Hill

US PoliticsNov 20, 2026

After the Shenzhen summit, Trump weighs another strike on Iran, reports say

The Nov. 19 Trump-Xi-Putin meeting in Shenzhen ended with a “peace declaration” that barely mentioned the Iran war, per Al Jazeera. Back in Washington, Trump is weighing another strike on Iran as shipping risks grow in the Strait of Hormuz, according to reports based on unnamed sources that are not yet confirmed. Envoy Jared Kushner said Trump believes peace in Ukraine is achievable.

Sources: Al Jazeera, investingLive

AsiaNov 20, 2026

Asian stocks plunge as the AI sell-off spreads

Japan's Nikkei 225 fell 5.06% to 56,436.65 and South Korea's Kospi dropped 5.21% to 4,869.96 on Friday as doubts about the AI trade hit technology shares. Asian equities were on track for their worst week since 2020.

Sources: CNBC, Yahoo Finance, Upstox

AsiaNov 20, 2026

Hang Seng closes at 22,410.80, near bear-market territory

Hong Kong's Hang Seng closed at 22,410.80. Sources put the drop between roughly 1.9% and 3.8% because they list different prior closes, and one outlet said the index is nearing a bear market.

Sources: Upstox, Kotak Neo, Yahoo Finance

CryptoNov 21, 2026

Bitcoin slips below $60,000, then rebounds

Bitcoin fell below $60,000 on Friday, with a 24-hour low reported near $55,100 (sources differ on the low), before rebounding toward $60,000 by Saturday morning in New York. One report put long liquidations at about $2 billion. Analysts cited record ETF outflows, the tech sell-off and talk of more Fed hikes.

Sources: crypto.news, CryptoBriefing, Around Prague

CryptoNov 21, 2026

Bitcoin clears its largest liquidation cluster

During the drop bitcoin cleared its largest liquidation cluster, between $59,000 and $62,000, according to CryptoBriefing, meaning many leveraged positions were forced out. A crypto.news analysis asks what would make the recovery above $60,000 last.

Sources: CryptoBriefing, crypto.news

ETFsNov 19, 2026

Spot bitcoin ETFs lose $582 million as IBIT posts a record outflow

U.S. spot bitcoin ETFs had net outflows of $582.3 million on Thursday, about $3.47 billion so far in November. BlackRock's IBIT lost $541.1 million on Nov. 19, its largest one-day outflow on record.

Sources: Around Prague (ETF flow data)

ETFsNov 20, 2026

SPY sees an $11.6 billion outflow as investors pull from index funds

The SPDR S&P 500 ETF (SPY) had an estimated $11.63 billion outflow on Nov. 18, with QQQ down $1.15 billion and XLE $727.9 million, per daily flow data. On Friday QQQ fell about 5.7% to close at 608.45.

Sources: GuruFocus, StockAnalysis

MacroNov 20, 2026

Markets price a December Fed hike at nearly 100%

CME FedWatch put the odds of a quarter-point hike at the Dec. 8-9 meeting at roughly 97% to 99% depending on the source, and a half-point move is now seen as unlikely. Minutes of the October meeting showed nearly all officials expected more increases. The 10-year Treasury yield fell about 10 basis points to near 4.94%.

Sources: Kitco, 24/7 Wall St., Admiral Markets

MacroNov 20, 2026

Consumer sentiment rises and inflation expectations ease

The University of Michigan's final November sentiment index rose to 51.5 from 48.0, its highest since August. One-year inflation expectations fell to 3.7% from 4.7% in October, and longer-term expectations eased to 3.3% from 3.5%.

Sources: Kitco

CommoditiesNov 20, 2026

Gold nears $3,500, its lowest since September 2025

Spot gold traded near $3,537.90 at 5 p.m. ET, down 2.38%, after touching $3,502.93, while silver fell 3.83% to about $41.12. Metals did not benefit from lower Treasury yields as investors sold across assets.

Sources: Kitco

CommoditiesNov 20, 2026

Oil rebounds on Friday but posts a third weekly loss

WTI settled at $83.98 (+0.93%) and Brent at $92.57 (+0.64%). On the week WTI lost 5.17% and Brent 3.41%, their third straight weekly decline, as Hormuz exports recover and U.S. inventories rise. Houthi warnings and mine incidents in the strait supported Friday's bounce.

Sources: EnergyNow (Reuters data)

CompaniesNov 20, 2026

Nvidia plunges about 14% as Anthropic's IPO fails to calm the AI rout

Nvidia fell about 14% to $125.53 after Reuters reported that the SEC is reviewing possible accounting issues, even though it had reported revenue of $115.8 billion, up 103%. The stock is roughly 43% to 47% below its October peak, depending on the source. Anthropic's newly listed shares fell about 9% in their second session, trading just above the $83 IPO price.

Sources: Motley Fool, tastylive, Axios, Fox Business

CompaniesNov 20, 2026

Intuit sinks on a weaker outlook; Walmart gains after earnings

Intuit fell more than 10% after cutting its fiscal 2027 revenue outlook and agreeing to sell Mailchimp for $3.68 billion. Walmart rose 2.1% to $128.73 after a narrow earnings beat, Ross Stores slipped on soft guidance and AMD lost about 13% amid the chip sell-off.

Sources: CNBC, Investing.com, Longbridge
